# 2026 CFF Technical Oversight Committee Election Results

Many thanks to all the nominees and everyone who voted in the election! 

Congratulations to Beyhan Veli (SAP), who has been re-elected to a two year term to the Technical Oversight Committee!

I’m pleased to announce our new TOC member will be:

* Matthew Kocher (Broadcom)

They'll be joining Stephan Merker (SAP), Greg Cobb (Broadcom), and Chris Weibel (Cloud.gov), whose seats will be up for election next year.

Congratulations to Beyhan and Matthew!

## Details

Actual votes cast: 104

Complete results of the election, using the Schulze rules for Condorcet completion, are:

* Matthew Kocher
* Beyhan Veli
* Ruben Koster

Records of all cast ballots are available in [CSV format here](https://github.com/cloudfoundry/community/blob/main/elections/2026/TOC/ballots.csv)
